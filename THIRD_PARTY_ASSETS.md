## Quaternius characters — CC0 1.0

The following free Standard editions were downloaded from the artist's official pages. Original license notices are retained in `assets/characters/*-LICENSE.txt`.

| Pack | Files used | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal Base Characters | Female and male base mesh, long hair, simple parted hair, textures | https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html |
| Modular Character Outfits — Fantasy | Female Ranger and Male Ranger outfits and textures | https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html |
| Universal Animation Library | Standard GLB animation library; seven clips are retargeted offline by `scripts/pack-characters.mjs` | https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html |

Local adaptations: mesh selection, head/neck and leg cropping, facial/waist adjustments, skin/hair/costume material changes, boots/shorts material regions, custom cloth face mask and cape, weapon attachment, animation retargeting and layered aiming. Textures were resized and encoded as WebP for delivery; duplicate textures were consolidated. The underlying character meshes and source animations are by Quaternius, not original project artwork.

In v108, Wraith reuses the existing Male Ranger outfit, male base character, simple parted hair, and retargeted animation library as a visible-faced twilight traveller. Local adaptations hide the original pauldron and hood, tint the cloth and leather, recolor the short hair silver-gray, and add a project-generated short scarf, moon clasp, cape, and shadow weapon displays. The former procedural `wraith-model.js` character was replaced by the shared skinned-character system. No new external assets or dependencies were introduced for this replacement.

In v109, Wraith retains that full character rig and animation system, re-enables the original skinned Ranger hood, and omits the short hair. Local adaptations add a fitted cool-gray death mask with dark eye sockets and narrow cyan eye slits, darken the clothing to black-violet, lengthen the project-generated cape with an irregular torn hem, and adjust the shadow weapon palette. The hood retains its authored skin weights; the new mask and cape details are generated by project code. This change uses the same existing CC0 assets and introduces no new external assets or dependencies.

In v110, Wraith uses the subtitle RIFT WALKER. Project-generated surface details replace the skull-like mask with a black cloth veil, a single asymmetric moon-white fissure, and subdued eye lines. Local adaptations use ink-black and blue-gray cloth, a small broken silver clasp, and an obliquely torn cape hem. The generated weapons use compressed dark fragments, discontinuous pale blade edges, and dark book pages with narrow white symbols; shadow combat effects use black silhouettes and pale cuts. The existing Quaternius character rig, hood, and animation assets remain in use. These are local code and palette changes with no new external assets or dependencies.

In v112, the same Wraith character has three weapon-dependent appearances while retaining the rounded Ranger hood and fractured cloth mask: a traveller with a narrow long scarf, diagonal chest cloth and hand-local shadow traces for Shade; an assassin with short shoulder cloth, a short cape and waist/back trails for Shadowblade; and a mage with an open V-shaped collar, a long cape, split front robe panels and slowly rising hem traces for Grimoire. The front robe panels attach at the pelvis, remain gravity-aligned, and sway lightly with the stride instead of being rigidly attached to the thighs. The scarf, collar, cape, robe panels and soft shadow shaders are local project-generated adaptations in `shadow-appearance.js`, `skinned-hero.js` and `shadow-aura.js`. They reuse the existing Quaternius CC0 body, outfit and animation assets; no new external or paid assets were added. These are equipment appearances, not new heroes or weapons: the roster remains seven heroes and thirteen weapons, with unchanged combat values and upgrade rules.

The hashed delivery GLBs use lossless gzip compression. Baked animation tracks preserve every retargeted Float32 sample. Original source assets and an uncompressed compatibility path are retained.

The game does not contain extracted models, textures or audio from Ori, NieR, Chainsaw Man, Rozen Maiden, or other commercial reference works.

## Current Lingya head — Quaternius Universal Base, CC0 1.0

Starting with v180, Lingya uses an independent head extracted from the repository's `silver-base.gltf`, part of the Quaternius Universal Base Characters pack credited above. The original female base, eye texture and eyebrow mesh are CC0; see `assets/characters/universal-base-characters-LICENSE.txt`.

Local adaptations: head/neck extraction, continuous rest-space proportions, soft skin vertex colours, native brown eyes, and a connected socket-surface Blink morph. In v181 the scalp-derived hair cap, locally generated twin plaits and fringe replace the old bob; a cream blouse, terracotta short overalls, round boots and satchel replace the moss-green outfit. Existing bones, gameplay attachment points and animation interfaces remain compatible. This replacement does not use the Rain facial mesh.

Reproduction: run `python scripts/prepare-lingya-head.py`, then `node scripts/pack-characters.mjs`. Current fallback files are `assets/characters/lingya-face.gltf` and `.bin`; `character-assets.js` identifies their active gzip delivery file. The same preparation step exports `lingya-hair-crown.js` from the CC0 scalp, used by the procedural plaits/fringe in `lingya-hair.js`. Shared Quaternius source files are not modified.

## Ground and stone surface textures — CC0 1.0

The four files in `assets/environment/` are unmodified 1K JPG maps from Poly Haven: [Forest Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor) (eye-candy.xyz) and [Rock 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_01), distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Source pages and file checksums were checked on 2026-10-10 and recorded in `assets/environment/sources.json`.

Each set supplies its diffuse and displacement map. Runtime shaders mix the diffuse map into the existing biome palette and use the displacement map only for surface-normal relief: playable terrain is not displaced. Forest litter is limited to forest/organic coastal and confluence ground; stone uses blended projections on obstacle boulders and selected ruin stones. These are real surface textures, not scanned environment models. Files are served locally, shared across maps and included in the offline bundle; failed image loading retains the previous procedural surface. Total delivered images: 2,880,466 bytes.

## Scanned environment models — Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (v213)

The following three models were downloaded from Poly Haven's official model/API endpoints on 2026-10-18. Their meshes, UVs and texture maps are external artwork; they are not models newly authored by this project. Poly Haven distributes these assets under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Delivered file in `assets/environment/models/` | Original model | Author | Delivered size | LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 triangles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `weathered-rock-a.glb` | [Rock 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07) | Jenelle van Heerden | 252,188 bytes | 1,800 / 700 / 179 |
| `weathered-rock-b.glb` | [Rock 09](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_09) | Jenelle van Heerden | 250,108 bytes | 1,800 / 699 / 180 |
| `old-stump.glb` | [Tree Stump 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_stump_02) | Rob Tuytel | 384,536 bytes | 1,800 / 700 / 180 |

Local adaptations in Blender 4.5.9 LTS: import the source glTF without executing source Blender scripts, bake source transforms, centre the horizontal origin, place the base at ground level, normalize the horizontal radius to approximately 1, smooth the surface and decimate three independent LOD meshes. Decimation slightly changes vertex positions; the largest delivered horizontal radius is approximately 1.01779, not an exact unit collider. The original UV layout is retained. Base colour, OpenGL normal and ARM (ambient occlusion/roughness/metalness) maps are resized from 1K to 512-pixel JPEGs and embedded in each GLB. Rock base-colour saturation is reduced by 40%; roughness is remapped to `0.62 + 0.38 × source`; ambient occlusion strength is 0.65 and normal strength is 0.75. Each asset uses one shared PBR material across its three LODs. Runtime biome colouring and placement adapt the models to the existing art direction; collision dimensions remain controlled by the existing game world.

The three delivery GLBs total **886,832 bytes** (about 0.85 MiB). Each contains three overlapping alternative meshes: render exactly one LOD at a time. Textures and buffers are embedded; no Draco, meshopt, KTX decoder or external CDN is needed. `sources.json` records the original asset/API URLs, authors, retrieval date, source and derivative hashes, transformations and per-LOD measurements. `LICENSE.txt` retains the attribution summary and `CC0-1.0.txt` the original CC0 legal text.

Reproduction: `python scripts/prepare-environment-assets.py --blender "/path/to/blender"`. Downloaded source glTF/bin/JPG files and original license evidence remain in the workspace's `outputs/art-upgrade-v213/sources/environment/`, outside the shipping repository. The script produces only the three selected GLBs and their provenance/license files in the runtime directory. `scripts/build-offline.mjs` explicitly includes those six files in both the service-worker cache and complete disk export; unselected models, source archives and Blender projects are not added by a recursive scan.

## Hero surface rebuilds — Quaternius CC0 sources and local Blender work (v215)

The seven playable heroes retain the existing Quaternius CC0 body/outfit foundations and 65 named bones. Local Blender reconstruction changes selected clothing, hoods, masks, hair and facial surfaces; it does not replace the animation library or create seven newly downloaded characters. See [the implementation record](docs/characters-v215.md) and [the exact delivery/source manifest](assets/characters/rebuilt-v215/sources.json).

The only newly downloaded external meshes adopted in this release are **Hair_Buzzed** and **Hair_Beard**, from the free Standard edition of [Quaternius Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Selected entries were downloaded from the artist's official itch.io delivery on 2026-10-18. The author's notice remains in `assets/characters/universal-base-characters-LICENSE.txt`; `assets/characters/rebuilt-v215/LICENSE.txt` records this release's provenance.

Blender adaptations combine the short hair and beard into the ranger's existing head slot, fit a low natural crest and close beard, add restrained brown vertex colours, smooth and decimate, then transfer normalized weights from the existing head. The delivered `scout-head-bfb5e5fa2c98.glb` contains 1,491 triangles, 868 vertices, one material and no texture images; 91,120 bytes. The existing face, eyes, skeleton, animations and weapon attachments remain in use. Source hashes and other delivered GLB hashes are recorded in the manifest above.

The other six heroes and the ranger clothing use previously bundled CC0 foundations, with project-authored surface reconstruction performed in Blender. The earlier [Fantasy Outfits](https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html) notice remains in `assets/characters/modular-character-outfits-fantasy-LICENSE.txt`. Previously bundled masks, patterned materials and project-generated details retain their existing origins. No models, textures or animations were extracted from commercial reference games.

Downloaded `Male_Peasant` and `Hair_BuzzedFemale` were evaluated but not integrated. `Female_Peasant`, `Hair_Buns` and `Hair_Long` are re-downloaded validation copies of resources already used by the project, not new asset introductions. Unused files and full source archives are outside the shipping repository.

The active v215 manifest contains 12 local GLBs totaling **5,769,436 bytes**; no new runtime textures. Existing local character textures are reused where applicable. Reproduction uses `scripts/hero-blender-tools.py`, the five `prepare-*` scripts listed in `assets/characters/rebuilt-v215/sources.json`, and `scripts/pack-hero-refinements.mjs`. Blender 4.5.9 LTS and the existing MIT-licensed [MCP for Blender](https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender) were verified locally; no paid generation service was used. Toolchain tests are not additional game assets.
