v217 locally adapts approved existing free character surfaces in Blender.
These local project faces, masks and clothes are not official Quaternius
characters or animations. No paid Source edition or new external character
has been adopted in this revision. The source archives actually available
are the free Standard Superhero male/female bases and male/female Ranger
and Peasant outfits; the product pages' larger full-pack counts are not
claimed as downloaded or licensed paid content.

See sources.json for the exact selected files, hashes, authoring scripts,
and retained v215/v216 surfaces. Previous revision notices remain intact.

The initial Lingya outfit adaptation is authored by
scripts/prepare-lingya-v217.py from the existing Female Ranger and female
Superhero rest surfaces. It changes continuous shirt/sleeve construction and
shorts tailoring while retaining original uncovered limb positions and rig.
Her previously delivered head and facial expression assets remain selected.
